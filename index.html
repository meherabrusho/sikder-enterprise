Turnkey Residential, Commercial & Institutional Buildings

EHS & Workforce Standards

Zero-Tolerance Safety & Labour Law Compliance

Working under major EPC primes like L&T requires strict daily compliance. We enforce the same PPE and workforce verification standards on every project.

Zero-Tolerance PPE Policy Mandatory helmets, high-visibility vests, safety boots, harnesses at height, and grinding/welding face shields.
No Underage Labour Strict age and national ID verification prior to onboarding, in full compliance with Bangladesh Labour Law.
Pre-Deployment Screening Documented worker verification and security gate-pass registration in line with prime-contractor onboarding protocols.
Daily Briefings & Housekeeping Pre-shift hazard checks and dedicated housekeeping crews to keep active work zones organized and clear.
